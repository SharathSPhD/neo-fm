# Licensable Kannada song-form sources (decision list for S)

Nothing here is contacted or paid for. Terms are what each kind of rights holder normally offers; no source has been asked, so every row's actual price and scope is **unverified until someone asks**. Public-domain Kannada song text is thin (Purandara Dasa is 4 Wikisource pages, Kanaka Dasa about 260 kirtanas), so volume has to come from a licence.

| Source | What it holds | Likely terms | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| IPRS (Indian Performing Right Society) | Lyric and composition rights for most film and album writers | Blanket licence for public performance and reproduction; ML-training use is not a standard product, so expect a bespoke deal | Single counterparty for the writers' share; recording rights are separate |
| Kannada label catalogues (Anand Audio, Lahari, Saregama/HMV, Aditya, Jhankar, PRK) | Film and album masters plus lyrics for the label's songs | Negotiated per catalogue; sync and "AI/data" licences are new, so ask for a research-only pilot first | Best fit for a taste corpus; Saregama has the oldest Kannada film catalogue |
| Dasa Sahitya / Haridasa archives (temple trusts, Kannada University Hampi, Karnataka Sangeetha Nritya Academy) | Edited Purandara, Kanaka, Vijaya, Gopala dasa kirtanas with tunes and ragas | Usually permission letters for non-commercial use; commercial reuse of the modern edition needs the editor/publisher | The compositions are PD but the scholarly editions and notation are not |
| Kannada Sahitya Parishat and university presses (Kuvempu, DVG, Bendre anthologies) | Bhavageete poets' collections | Publisher licence per book; many poets are still in copyright (Bendre d.1981, Kuvempu d.1994, DVG d.1975) | Bendre/Kuvempu/DVG are not PD until 2041/2054/2035 |
| Akashvani / Prasar Bharati archives | Decades of Kannada bhavageete, light music and folk broadcasts | Archive access by request; reuse needs the rights of the performers and writers on top | Rich, but rights per item are unclear |
| Karnataka Janapada Parishat / folklore archives | Folk songs, collected from oral tradition | Often CC or permission-based for scholarly use; collector's compilation rights apply | Good for authenticity and regional variety |
| Internet Archive Kannada 78rpm and Digital Library of India | Pre-1965 recordings and out-of-copyright books | Recording term lapsed (tier P); composition rights still need clearing | Already catalogued as tier P in ia.jsonl |

## Recommendation
Ask one label catalogue (Saregama, oldest Kannada film catalogue) and IPRS for a written research/pilot licence covering lyrics for model-conditioning, not republication, and in parallel ask the Dasa Sahitya custodians for a permission letter. All three are asks for S to approve; none has been sent.
